📖 Yarra School Self-Evaluation Record — Purpose & Guidelines

Purpose

This process is not about proving that your school is good. It is about helping your leadership team answer questions such as:

  • What are we doing well?
  • Where are we inconsistent?
  • What should we focus on next?
  • What will make the biggest difference over the next five years?

The more honest your reflections, the more useful the evaluation will be.

Guidelines for Self Evaluation

Focus on the whole school. Rate each indicator based on what is seen across the school, not on isolated examples of excellent practice.

Use evidence wherever possible. Base your ratings on observations, data, documents and regular practices rather than perceptions or intentions.

Consider consistency. Ask whether the practice is evident across classrooms, teams or departments, or only in a few areas.

Reflect on current practice. Evaluate what is happening today rather than what is planned or intended for the future.

Involve different perspectives. Where possible, discuss the indicators with leaders, teachers and relevant staff to develop a shared understanding of current practice.

Be realistic. Every school has areas of strength and areas for improvement. Honest ratings create the strongest foundation for meaningful school improvement.

Use the reflection questions. The comments and evidence sections are as important as the ratings — they provide context and help identify priorities for future action.
